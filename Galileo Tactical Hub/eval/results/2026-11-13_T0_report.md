## Fragmentation investigation (2026-11-15)

The tracks_per_player FAIL (1.53, limit 1.52) is **not shown to be a regression**:

1. **Libraries ruled out.** `track` rerun with 2023-era libraries (numpy 1.26.4, scipy 1.11.4,
   scikit-learn 1.3.2, opencv 4.8.1) on the same clip gives every number identical (1.53, purity 0.890).
   Kit KMeans is seeded and runs after tracking; OpenCV 4 vs 5 only moved the position median.
2. **The baseline is too coarse to compare.** The metric is video tracks / provider players seen =
   29 / 19 = 1.526. With 19 players it moves in steps of 1/19 = 0.053: the only values near 1.5 are
   28/19 = 1.474 and 29/19 = 1.526. The baseline was recorded at one decimal ("1.5 tracks per player"
   in CHANGES.md), which both round to. So "<= 1.52" really means "<= 28 tracks", and whether the
   baseline run had 28 or 29 is unknown.
3. **Where the 10 extra tracks come from** (`eval/diag_fragmentation.py`, log `logs/T0_fragmentation.log`):

| Cause | Count | Players (provider index) |
|---|---|---|
| Split across the 2 s close-up shot (8-10 s video, no pitch view) | 4 | 1, 3, 7, 10 |
| Zero-gap identity handover (track ends, another starts the next frame while visible) | 4 | 8, 17, 20, 23 |
| One-frame break while in view | 1 | 1 |
| Re-entry after 3.9 s out of view | 1 | 17 |

`stitch` allows gaps up to 4 s, so the close-up splits could in principle be joined; with 18.5 m reach
after 2 s and several same-team players leaving together, the greedy stitch has many candidates. Fixing
this belongs to T4 (tracker bake-off), which is gated on IDF1 and ID switches.

**Proposed gate fix (needs Keno):** state the gate as a count on the frozen synthetic clip, e.g.
"<= 29 video tracks over 19 provider players", re-recorded from this machine at full precision, so a
one-track change is visible and the limit is not hidden by rounding.
