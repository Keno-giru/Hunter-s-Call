## Decisions (also in ROADMAP.md decision log)
- SoccerNet-GSR is the exam source (broadcast, human labels in Galileo's 4 classes, ids, pitch lines); Kaggle drone/fisheye sets kept for later.
- Per-frame H from named lines + circles; GSR's own player positions used only as a 50 px gross-error guard (they sit 20-30 px off the feet).
- SNGS-119 dropped (inconsistent side names in its first 10 s).
- Keep >= 6 landmarks per frame (Keno).
- Review in self-hosted CVAT; a completed job counts as reviewed; per-clip review mode full or sample (Keno).
